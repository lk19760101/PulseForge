Vision

A photograph the clinic already takes.

The retina is the only place in the body where blood vessels and nerve tissue can be observed directly, without an incision and without contrast. A fundus camera captures that view in seconds.

Most of what those images contain is never read. The equipment is already installed in ophthalmology clinics, diabetes programmes and screening camps. What is missing is the capacity to interpret every image that passes through them.

NetraX is built for that gap — to read one photograph for signals that would otherwise require separate tests, separate appointments and separate journeys for the patient.
